Key findings

Is the LLM judge trustworthy?

Every failure-mode verdict in this benchmark comes from an LLM judge. We checked it against an independent human annotator on 19 randomly sampled pipelines (285 individual ratings).

89.5%raw agreement
0.62Cohen's κ (95% CI 0.50–0.73)
0.71judge precision
0.65judge recall

Precision is higher than recall throughout: when the judge flags a failure it's usually right, but it misses some real ones — so the failure rates reported in this benchmark are more likely an under-estimate than an over-estimate.
